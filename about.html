CHAPTER 01

The Akihabara Alleyway to Central Vault

PokéVault Legends began in 2020 with a single quest: eliminating counterfeit fear from the Pokémon collecting community. As vintage card prices surged into five and six-figure valuations, the marketplace became flooded with resealed booster boxes, counterfeit PSA cases, and unlicensed bootlegs.

Our founders — lifelong collectors who spent years scouring Tokyo's Akihabara card shops and Kyoto antique markets — formed a coalition with senior forensic appraisers. What started as a private collection safehouse evolved into South Asia's premier authenticated marketplace and climate-controlled vault storage facility.
